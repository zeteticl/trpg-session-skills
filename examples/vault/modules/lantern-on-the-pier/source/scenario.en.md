# Lantern on the Pier (original short module)

> Original example for this toolkit. Unofficial; not a Chaosium product.

## Pitch

1920s harbor city. Three dockworkers share the same dream of a lantern that should not be lit. At dawn the lantern hangs at the end of an abandoned pier — and nobody admits hanging it.

## Hooks

A reporter, harbor clerk, or priest is asked by foreman Ah-Sing to find who hung the lantern and calm the crew before a strike.

## Scenes

1. **Shed briefing** — nightmares and no-shows.
2. **Newspaper morgue** — clipping: a drowning twelve years ago on the same pier; the dead carried a lantern.
3. **The pier** — lantern, tide smell, locked locker with a photo handout.
4. **Endings** — take it down / keep vigil / cast it into the sea.

## Clues

- Clipping surname matches the foreman’s distant relatives.
- Photo verso: “Don’t let it watch the shore.”
- Tide-like marks under the lantern; not local craft.

## NPCs

- **Ah-Sing** — foreman, fear over honesty.
- **Chen Wei** — archive assistant, wants the scoop.
- **The Lantern Bearer** — never on-stage; dreams and tide only.

## System notes (CoC)

- Close sight of the lantern: SAN 0/1d3.
- Understanding the marks: SAN 1/1d4.
- No forced combat; late-night linger may become a chase of phantom tide-sounds.
